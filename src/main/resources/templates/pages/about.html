Our Story · Barotac Nuevo

Rooted in Ilonggo kitchens, designed for today's table.

Located right along Burgos-Osmeña St in Barotac Nuevo, Iloilo, Ilonggo Bistro celebrates the comforting dishes we grew up loving — steaming bowls of La Paz Batchoy, smoky Chicken Inasal, slow-braised KBL, and freshly whisked Iloilo tablea — served in a bright, welcoming contemporary bistro setting.

Our doors open early at 7:00 AM for travelers, families, and coffee lovers, and stay warm until 9:00 PM for unhurried dinners with friends.
